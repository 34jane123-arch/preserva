About PRESERVA

Connecting local produce sellers with people who want affordable food.

Good food deserves a buyer.

Every day, fresh fruits and vegetables go unsold in local markets. PRESERVA helps sellers in Panabo City find buyers for that produce before it spoils, at a price that works for everyone.

🥀

The problem

Farmers and produce sellers often can't sell their remaining fruits and vegetables before they spoil. That means wasted food and lost income.

🌱

Our solution

PRESERVA lets sellers list produce at a rescue price, so buyers can find it and visit the stall before it goes bad.

Our mission

Support local sellers, make produce easier to find, and encourage responsible use of food.

📍

Where we are

Panabo City, Davao del Norte, Philippines

🌍

Our goal

Contribute to UN Sustainable Development Goal 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.
