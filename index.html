eco About the PreServa Vision

PRESERVA — Sell Fresh. Save More. Waste Less.

PRESERVA is an agricultural technology platform designed to help local market vendors reduce food waste while making fresh and affordable produce accessible to the community. It connects vendors with local buyers and businesses through freshness-based discounts, imperfect but safe produce listings, and product reservations.

Through PRESERVA, we aim to give unsold produce another opportunity to be sold instead of going to waste, helping vendors recover their costs and allowing buyers to enjoy affordable products.

track_changes
Target Milestone

Our Goal

To reduce fruit and vegetable spoilage among local market vendors by providing an accessible platform where unsold and surplus produce can be sold at discounted prices before they spoil. PRESERVA aims to help vendors earn, buyers save, and communities minimize food waste.

flag
Our Mandate

Our Mission

To connect local vendors, household buyers, and businesses through a convenient and affordable digital platform that promotes responsible purchasing, reduces food waste, supports local livelihoods, and makes fresh produce more accessible to everyone.

visibility
Long-Term Future

Our Vision

To become a trusted agricultural technology platform that helps build a community where no good produce goes to waste, local vendors thrive, and everyone has access to affordable and quality food.
